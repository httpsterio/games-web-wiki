# Monetization

Not current work. Plan for later.

- Subscriptions are the main business. A one time lifetime unlock is offered alongside, priced so it's an easy choice.
- More games join the pool over time, which is what the subscription pays for.
- A free trial run by the game itself, not by the stores.
- A locked down free tier.
- Leaning towards no ads.
- One entitlement, premium, granted by any of them.
- RevenueCat in front of Google Play, the App Store and web payments.
- Web payments through Paddle. Paddle is the merchant of record and handles EU VAT. Card data never touches our
  servers.
- Native wrappers: Android first, iOS if it makes financial sense. iPhone haptics need Capacitor.
